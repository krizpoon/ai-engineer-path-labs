# AI Engineer Path privacy policy

*Last updated: 3 November 2026*

This policy covers the AI Engineer Path app for iPhone and iPad.

We do not receive your reading progress, quiz results or any other personal data. The app has no sign-in, no advertising and no analytics.

**On your device.** The app saves which chapters you have completed, your best quiz scores and the last chapter you opened, so you can continue where you left off. This information stays on your device, is not included in device backups, and is deleted when you delete the app.

**Links.** Optional links open third-party websites, including documentation, certification providers and GitHub. Those services handle information under their own privacy policies.

**Children.** The app is a course for software engineers and is not directed at children.

**Changes.** If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new date.

**Contact.** Questions about this policy: [ai-engineer@twinkle.hk](mailto:ai-engineer@twinkle.hk).
