# Staff handbook (sample)

## Expenses

Staff may book standard-class rail travel for client visits. Keep every receipt and submit claims within 30 days. Claims above £500 need a director's approval.

## Leave

Full-time staff get 25 days of annual leave plus public holidays. Parental leave is 26 weeks at full pay for the primary carer and 4 weeks for the secondary carer.

## Equipment

Laptops are replaced every three years. Report a lost or stolen laptop to the IT desk within 24 hours so it can be locked remotely.
